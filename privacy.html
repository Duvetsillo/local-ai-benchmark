PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Local AI Benchmark is designed to operate locally and to minimize data collection. We do not sell personal data and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

1. Information we collect

We only collect the minimum information needed to respond to a contact or support request. This may include your name, email address, model of interest, and a short description of your project or use case.

2. How we use information

We use personal information only to answer questions, provide support, and evaluate legitimate inquiries about the service. We do not use your information for marketing without your explicit opt-in.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies, third-party analytics, or cross-site tracking. We may use a simple local consent cookie to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies.

4. Local data and benchmark runs

Benchmark results are generated locally on the device running the project. Any output, prompt text, or hardware data remains under the control of the operator. We do not automatically upload benchmark results to our servers.

5. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data. Contact us using the details in the website footer to exercise those rights.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@localaibenchmark.com.
