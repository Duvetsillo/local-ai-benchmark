Resources and consent

Consent and minimum data collection

We only request information that is necessary to answer a legitimate inquiry or provide support. No unnecessary personal data is requested.

Analytics and third-party integrations

We do not currently use third-party analytics, ad networks, or user tracking scripts. If any third-party service is added in the future, it will be disclosed in this policy and consent will be obtained before activation where required.

Accessibility

We aim to provide keyboard access, clear labels, semantic HTML, visible focus states, and sufficient contrast. We also provide alternative text for meaningful non-decorative visual elements.

Local legal compliance

Use of local AI tools and benchmark workflows must comply with relevant laws in your country or region, including privacy, data protection, copyright, and software licensing obligations.

Important risk notice

Benchmark results are local and contextual. They do not guarantee that a model will perform similarly in a different environment or workload. Results should be interpreted as evidence for your current setup, not as universal rankings.
